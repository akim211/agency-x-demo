Drought Relief Grant Application

The Drought Relief Grant Program provides financial assistance to Indiana farmers affected by drought conditions. Eligible applicants may receive up to $15,000 to cover crop loss, livestock feed, and water infrastructure costs.

📋 Application Period: June 1 – September 30, 2026
